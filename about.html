Our Mission

Combining Timeless Financial Wisdom with Practical Tools

BalanceLedger exists to make intentional income planning accessible, transparent, and culturally inclusive—no sign-ups, no spreadsheets required.

Why We Built BalanceLedger

Most personal finance apps treat money purely as a Western spreadsheet problem, ignoring regional currencies or ethical traditions that value generosity alongside wealth creation. At the same time, ancient teachings on livelihood and household stewardship—such as the Sigālovāda Sutta's 25/50/25 division—often remain locked inside dense philosophical texts without an instant tool to test them on a real paycheck.

BalanceLedger bridges that gap. By placing classical wisdom side-by-side with proven modern frameworks like the 50/30/20 Rule, the 6 Jars Method, the 80/20 Rule, and the 70/20/10 Rule, we empower households, freelancers, and business owners around the world to compare strategies in seconds.

Built for Global Currencies & Privacy

Financial clarity should never require sharing sensitive income numbers with a third-party server or linking your bank login. Every calculation on BalanceLedger runs strictly inside your browser with support for eight major currencies across Asia, Europe, and North America: USD, MMK, THB, SGD, INR, EUR, GBP, and JPY.

Zero Server Storage

Your monthly income figures stay on your device. Nothing is logged, stored in a database, or transmitted when you calculate or copy your plan.

Educational Clarity

Each calculator page includes clear formulas, concrete worked examples, and honest trade-offs so you can choose a method with confidence.
