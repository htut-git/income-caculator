Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026. Your privacy is central to BalanceLedger. This policy explains what information is—and is not—collected when you visit our website.

1. Calculator Inputs & Client-Side Processing

All budgeting calculations performed on BalanceLedger—including your monthly income numbers, currency selections, and method comparisons—execute 100% locally in your web browser via client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit, log, store, or sell the financial figures you enter into our calculators.

2. Log Files & Technical Information

Like most static websites and cloud hosting platforms (such as Cloudflare Pages or Netlify), our hosting provider may automatically record standard server log data when you request a page. This technical information may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring or exit pages, and aggregate click counts. This data is not linked to personally identifiable financial inputs and is used solely for site security, uptime monitoring, and aggregate traffic analysis.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

BalanceLedger does not set first-party tracking cookies to store your personal income calculations. However, third-party advertising partners or analytics tools integrated on the site may use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons to measure ad effectiveness and deliver relevant advertisements.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Ad Vendors

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising networks to keep our budgeting tools completely free for all visitors. Please note the following standard disclosures:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick or advertising cookies) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to BalanceLedger and/or other sites across the web.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

BalanceLedger has no access to or control over the cookies placed by third-party advertisers. Please consult the respective privacy policies of those third-party ad servers for more detailed information about their practices and opt-out instructions.

5. Contact Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact us via email or a contact form, we use your name, email address, and message content solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add visitors to unsolicited mailing lists or sell contact details to third parties.

6. Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction—including residents of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California—you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by a service, as well as the right to opt out of personalized advertising cookies. You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your individual browser settings or via the consent tools displayed where required by regional law.

7. Children's Information

BalanceLedger does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has submitted personal contact details through email, please contact us so we can promptly remove the correspondence.

8. Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. If you have questions about this policy, please visit our Contact page or email privacy@balanceledger.org.
